# Dev Trader execution audit — 4 October 2026

## Evidence

Before changes, the live service reported 19 open local execution records but Bitget reported two aggregate BTC positions (0.010 BTC long, 0.004 BTC short). Local recorded closed PnL was only -3.008 USDT. Available balance was 918.708 USDT; this is available collateral, not total account equity, and does not explain the user's reported $990 to $880 equity change. Do not infer the full drawdown from these values.

## Corrections

- Normalize UTA v3 current/history prices, times, realized/unrealized PnL and fee fields, plus instrument precision/minimum quantity.
- Match position-history lifecycles before treating a newer same-side position as an old trade's current position. Reject history closed before an entry. Mark entries with ambiguous aggregate partial exits RECONCILIATION_PENDING, report actual aggregate exposure/PnL once, and block new execution. Fail closed on unmatched exposure or degraded reconciliation.
- Do not import closing or canceled unfilled orders as new entries. Restore estimated stop risk for recovered opening orders.
- Preserve significant zeroes when formatting integer prices and quantities.
- Serialize order admission and enforce one unresolved position at a time, preventing additive pyramiding and opposite hedges.
- Include estimated opening/closing market fees in position sizing. Size against the current exchange quote and normalized stop. Reject executable net reward/risk below 1.5.
- Pause new entries after two consecutive daily losses or a 1% daily loss of available collateral; retain three-entry daily cap and 0.25% default risk. UTC counting is timezone independent; recovered exchange entries restore the cap after deployment.
- Require signal ID, Grade A, finite values and fresh timestamps when supplied. Validate replacements before closing positions.
- Block early momentum against the 15m trend; require at least baseline volume and directional flow.
- Correct repeated learning resolutions rather than counting the same outcome twice.
- Gate Android release publishing on backend CI success.
- Report candle-only backtesting as unavailable for profitability validation: the current live strategy requires synchronized historical order-flow and timeframe context absent from that replay.

## Validation and limits

84 backend regression tests passed and Python compilation succeeded locally after the expanded 0.12.0 audit. No test proves future profitability. Forward demo monitoring is required, including exchange net PnL, fees, expectancy and drawdown. Confidence is a heuristic score, not a calibrated win probability.

Existing server files default to /tmp and the free Render service has no persistent disk. A deployment can erase local execution/learning state. The engine reconstructs opening orders and outcomes from exchange history, but original strategy context may be unavailable after a restart. Do not call this durable learning. Recovered aggregate outcomes are apportioned by filled quantity; individual realized PnL attribution is approximate when several historical entries share a position lifecycle.

The historical $110 account decline is not fully attributed by this audit. The accounting fixes enable more reliable exchange reconciliation; a complete equity ledger including manual trades/transfers is needed to establish its cause.

## Expanded 0.12.0 corrections

- Fix the executor discarding the client's already-unwrapped instrument rules. Honor UTA quantityMultiplier and priceMultiplier, and block sizing when instrument configuration is unavailable.
- Read bid1Price/ask1Price, choose the executable side, reject spreads above 4 bps and crossed quotes.
- Derive deterministic client IDs within Bitget's length limit. Preserve an unresolved submission on ambiguous failures and recover it by client ID. A filled status without actual exchange quantity and average price cannot become a confirmed fill.
- Keep private execution requests out of the market-feed callback. Serialize reconciliation and prevent entry admission during an incomplete reconciliation cycle.
- Add a paginated 30-day fill ledger with execution-ID deduplication, fees/rebates, realized P&L curve, explicit completeness and FIFO-estimate labeling. Funding/transfers remain excluded; the ledger does not claim total account return.
- Observe account equity separately from collateral. Pause after 1% daily observed equity decline; halve risk following an observed peak drawdown above 2%. These anchors depend on retained local state.
- Recheck historical setup vetoes at execution. Require directional CVD/book confirmation for early momentum; OI rising alone is insufficient.
- Replace the Android dashboard with Trade, Positions and Insights workspaces, complete scrollable setup details, exchange accounting and entry-block explanations. Chart LIVE indicators now depend on verified feed health.
- Add six native emulator viewport checks to the publication gate. Preview candles/account values exist only in the debug source set and are checked absent from the release APK.
- The first native run caught a null fallback for optional signal style metadata. Correct it to an empty string, preserve bootstrap feed-source metadata, and display confirmed fills separately from planned entries. Failed emulator checks blocked the initial APK from being published.
- Enforce the daily loss limit using complete exchange fill P&L after fees when it shows a larger loss than reconciled local entry records; never add both representations together. This closes a bypass caused by ambiguous partial exits.

Backend 0.13.0 / refined-demo-v2 adds:

- Confirmed 15m range breakout plus next-bar retest, volume expansion and bounded live entry distance; wick-only, failed, incomplete and extended triggers are rejected. The candidate still passes all normal quality, historical and execution gates.
- Opposing 1h/day/week levels are checked before trusting an optimistic target. Fee-adjusted space to the first obstacle can veto an entry.
- Aligned 1h/4h trend and 15m structure provide an independent confirmation bucket, so continuation trades do not depend on an Elliott vote. Elliott/harmonic remain heuristic context; named harmonic patterns are not claimed as fully implemented.
- Candle-derived previous-day POC and current-session VWAP estimates. A full previous UTC day is required; an untouched estimate also requires complete current-day touch history. These are explicitly **not exact trade-level NPOC** and never manufacture a signal.
- Time-matched five-minute price/CVD comparison with sufficient observed duration and no large tape gaps. Candle movement is no longer compared with six unrelated recent ticks.
- Separate quote, trade and book freshness, concurrent history warmup, preservation of live CVD/forming bars, and reconnection after a silent socket. Quiet demo tape can remain usable for up to 15 seconds while quotes remain under 3 seconds and book under 5 seconds; REST-only continuity stays degraded.
- Public-channel diagnostics distinguish subscription acknowledgements from received market packets. A subscription acknowledgement never makes an old book fresh; the system check exposes channel errors and packet counts. Reduced freshness is reported as an entry block without incorrectly claiming that the active WebSocket has been replaced by a fallback feed.
- Independent exchange stop-quantity verification. Partial/duplicate/wrong-side/crossed/limit stops do not establish full protection. Missing protection is repaired with a full-position market stop; unresolved protection halts entries and attempts one verified remaining-quantity close. Read or submission uncertainty is never treated as proof of a safe fill or permission to retry blindly.
- Pending-order, incomplete accounting and expired-decision admission blocks. Position sizing respects the remaining verified daily loss budget and never rounds up to exchange minimum size.
- 32-character exchange client IDs recover strategy family, timeframe, regime and approximate planned risk after a restart. Outcomes use execution-ID aliases to avoid duplicate learning. Recovery is bounded by the engine's 30-day order-history window; complete original flow/thesis history is not durable on free temporary hosting.
- Session cutoff prevents old exchange entries or remote predictions from consuming new-session signal quota or reappearing as current exposure.
