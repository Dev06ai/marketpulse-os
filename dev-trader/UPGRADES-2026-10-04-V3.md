# Dev Trader 0.14.0 — market-decision-v3

## Decision behavior

- A causal daily/4h/1h/15m structure map records strict confirmed swings, their confirmation times, active/touched/broken/expired levels, closed-bar breaks and recent range boundaries. Duplicate hours, gaps and partial aggregates cannot become complete 4h or daily candles.
- One-hour observations control a regime selector. A change needs two distinct closed hourly observations; volatility shocks veto new entries immediately. Intrabar packets do not count as additional confirmations.
- Independent SFP reversal, breakout/retest, trend pullback, D-Line, MSS continuation and early-momentum playbooks have explicit admission and cancellation rules. One-hour context is central; the lower timeframe supplies entry timing. Continuation directions must agree with the selected condition and higher-timeframe context. SFPs need a nearby observed major level and independent flow confirmation.
- v3 executable evidence removes the previous Elliott score contribution and does not require harmonic/Elliott votes. Those fields remain experimental context. Evidence scores are heuristic and are not win probabilities.
- Existing execution protections remain: healthy/fresh quotes, trade tape and depth, grade/evidence/reward requirements, executable net reward/risk, one unresolved position, risk budgets, daily trade limits and loss pauses. No live-money route was added.

## Data and efficiency

- The hourly history now retains up to 720 bars. Previous daily/weekly highs and lows require complete contiguous UTC periods; a partial week is no longer presented as a complete previous-week level.
- Executed-trade profiles use price, size and execution-ID deduplication, fixed configurable price bins, UTC sessions and explicit demo/venue identity. Session VWAP and untouched prior-day POC are actionable only with complete observed coverage. Startups, disconnects, long silent periods and incomplete touch history are flagged. No REST live tape is mixed into the demo executed-volume profile.
- A prior-day profile cannot be complete immediately after deployment. It must collect a whole observed session without invalidating gaps. The profile is venue/session/bin specific, not an exact global Bitcoin volume distribution.
- Features are reused across detectors and mobile payloads. Cached values invalidate on candle corrections, price/flow/book changes and time boundaries. Structural history does not use future confirmed bars.

## Journal, replay and execution

- The bounded SQLite journal stores versioned accepted/rejected candidate decisions, market snapshots, one-second sampled prices, execution events and exchange-resolved outcomes. It exposes compact review, full export, causal structure inspection and chronological training/unseen windows.
- Replay is decision inspection. Sampled snapshots are not a complete trade-by-trade exchange replay and cannot establish strategy profitability.
- Exchange client IDs preserve v3 revision plus strategy family/timeframe/regime/planned risk within the existing 32-character limit. Retained journal context can restore a recovered exchange entry's original thesis while exchange position/order/history remains authoritative.
- Positive partial fills receive stop coverage checks immediately. Canceled orders with confirmed partial fills are managed as remaining real exposure rather than failed empty orders. Canceled zero-fill orders cannot be promoted to OPEN by an old position snapshot.
- Journal errors are observable and do not cause market-feed disconnection loops.

## Exit comparisons and evidence

- Three versioned shadow exit plans compare the same candidate: fixed final target; half at TP1 with trailing behind subsequently confirmed 1h swings; and a bounded time exit.
- Shadow estimates include configurable taker fees and adverse slippage. Stop gaps use observed prices, not guaranteed ideal fills. Incomplete paths are excluded; unresolved horizons are censored.
- These are same-signal exit and legacy admission-gate comparisons. They are not independently traded portfolios or a full previous-engine walk-forward backtest. No exit variant is automatically promoted based on a handful of observations.
- The exchange scorecard groups net results by playbook, regime and revision and reports net expectancy, drawdown and losing streaks. Unresolved trades and shadow estimates are excluded from realized profitability.

## Storage and operational limits

- The default journal remains in /tmp on the existing free service. It survives a process restart only when the local file survives; deployment/replacement can erase it. Export is available. A configured persistent volume/path is needed for durable full decision history. No paid resource was created, MarketPulse was not restarted, and no durability guarantee is made.
- Trade-volume profiles and regime hysteresis warm again after replacement. Exchange history/client IDs recover bounded execution context. Original decision context requires retained journal storage.
- Existing demo epoch/baseline, 0.25% default risk, three-entry ceiling, two-consecutive-loss pause and daily-loss accounting were not reset or increased.
- Profitability remains unproven. Unseen-period validation and forward demo results across different conditions are required. Thirty trades is only the scorecard's descriptive sample warning threshold, not proof of an edge.

## Validation

152 backend regressions passed before publication; Python compilation passed. New coverage includes causal swings, missing/duplicate aggregate hours, complete UTC day/week requirements, regime persistence, feature correction invalidation, executed-volume deduplication/coverage, stale/future feed rejection, playbook admission, costed shadow exits, gap exclusion, journal recovery/errors, causal replay, net scorecards and protection of partial fills. A synthetic full-state benchmark (720 hourly bars) averaged about 8.2 ms per evaluation locally; this is not a hosting latency guarantee.

This is a hosted-engine update compatible with Android build 98; no APK change is required.
