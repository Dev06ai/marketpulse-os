MARKETPULSE · QUESTIONS
Questions
A few straightforward answers so new traders can understand what MarketPulse is, what it does, and what it does not do.
Is this a signal group?
No. MarketPulse is a market-analysis and decision-support workspace. It presents market data, structure, flow and setup context so you can make your own trading decisions. It is not a chat group or a promise to tell you what to buy or sell.
Do you touch my money or connect directly to my exchange?
MarketPulse does not take custody of your funds. Any exchange or execution capability is subject to the controls, permissions and safety boundaries shown inside the product. Never give your exchange password or withdrawal credentials to anyone.
Will this make me a better trader?
The platform is designed to make market information easier to organize and review. Improvement still depends on your own practice, risk management, discipline and decision-making. No tool can guarantee profitable trading.
Which assets can I analyze?
Use the asset selector and the Market Scanner to see the instruments currently supported by the live data layer. Availability can change when a provider changes its coverage.
I've never used tools like these. Can I learn them here?
Yes. The Guide, beginner explanations, simple labels and plain-language descriptions are designed so you can start with the dashboard before exploring advanced concepts such as OI, CVD, funding and liquidations.
Do I need to be a full-time or day trader?
No. MarketPulse can be used for learning, market review, planning and trade journaling. Choose a timeframe and workflow that matches your own objectives rather than forcing a particular trading style.
What does MarketPulse actually show me?
The core workspace combines price action, market structure, futures flow, confluence, setup context, risk tools, watchlists and journaling. Advanced validation and infrastructure diagnostics are reserved for the owner/admin layer.
Where does the data come from?
MarketPulse combines live market data from connected providers with derived analytics. Source availability and coverage can vary, so data panels should be read with the source/status information shown by the product.

MarketPulse — Beginner Guide

Use the dashboard in this order. Advanced metrics are there for extra context; you do not need to memorize them.

1 · Start with the SignalThe top cards show price, market mode, the current signal and a confidence score. “No trade” means the signals are not aligned enough.
2 · Check the chartChoose 15m, 30m, 1h, 4h or 1D. Move over candles to see time and OHLC. Use the 50 EMA and volume for extra context.
3 · Check safetyMarket Flow shows futures positioning. Optional funded-account rules can be enabled separately; core market/data/validation safety gates always remain active.
Open interest (OI)How much futures position value is still open.
Buy / sell pressure (CVD)A view of whether aggressive market buying or selling is dominating.
FundingA periodic payment between long and short futures traders.
LiquidationsForced closing of positions when required margin is no longer available.
DrawdownHow far the current price is below a recent high.
Risk / rewardHow much potential gain there is relative to the amount you plan to risk.
