MarketPulse — Beginner Guide

Use the dashboard in this order. Advanced metrics are there for extra context; you do not need to memorize them.

1 · Start with the SignalThe top cards show price, market mode, the current signal and a confidence score. “No trade” means the signals are not aligned enough.
2 · Check the chartChoose 15m, 30m, 1h, 4h or 1D. Move over candles to see time and OHLC. Use the 50 EMA and volume for extra context.
3 · Check safetyMarket Flow shows futures positioning. The prop-firm and event checks turn your own rules into simple pass, caution or blocked results.
Open interest (OI)How much futures position value is still open.
Buy / sell pressure (CVD)A view of whether aggressive market buying or selling is dominating.
FundingA periodic payment between long and short futures traders.
LiquidationsForced closing of positions when required margin is no longer available.
DrawdownHow far the current price is below a recent high.
Risk / rewardHow much potential gain there is relative to the amount you plan to risk.
